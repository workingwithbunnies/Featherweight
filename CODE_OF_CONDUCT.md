# Code of conduct

Featherweight is a small, friendly project, and many people here are new to GitHub or to how Windows works.
Everyone is welcome.

## Expected

- Be patient and kind, especially with beginners. There are no stupid questions.
- Assume good intentions, and give helpful, specific feedback.
- Keep discussions about the project and its users.

## Not accepted

- Insults, harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Posting other people's private information.
- Spam, or promoting unrelated products.

## What happens

The maintainers may edit or remove comments, close discussions, or block people who don't follow this code.
To report a problem with someone's behaviour, use the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** form
(it's private) or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.
